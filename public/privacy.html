G<Logger privacy policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

G<Logger is a time logger that saves your sessions to your own Google Calendar. This page explains what it accesses and why.

What the app accesses

Google Calendar. The app creates a calendar (named “Log” by default) and adds, edits and deletes the sessions you log there. It reads events from that calendar only to show your timeline and history.

Google Drive app data. Your settings (categories, colours, icons and the calendar name) are kept in a hidden app-data file in your Drive. The app cannot see any of your other Drive files.

Your email address. Used only to show which account you're signed in with.

What is stored, and where

Your log and settings stay in your own Google account. G<Logger does not store, sell or share your log. To keep you signed in, an encrypted sign-in token is kept in a secure cookie in your browser; the server holds nothing about you. Some display preferences (like dark mode) are saved in your browser.

Reminders

If you turn on the long-timer reminder, the server stores your device's notification address, your reminder setting, and the start time and category of a running timer, so it can tell you when a timer has run long. Turning reminders off deletes this.

Removing access

Tap Sign out in Settings, or remove G<Logger at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Your logged events stay in your calendar until you delete them.

Google API data

G<Logger's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
